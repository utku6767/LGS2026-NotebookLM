## 1. Soru Tipi: Unit 1 - Friendship / Inviting & Refusing with a Reason

**Soru Kökü:** 
**Jack:** Hey friends! I am organizing a movie night at my house this Saturday. Would you like to join me?  
* **Andy:** I'd love to, but I have to finish my science project.  
* **Betty:** That sounds awesome! I'll definitely be there.  
* **Carl:** I'm sorry, but I can't. My grandparents are visiting us.  
* **David:** No, thanks. I don't like watching movies.  

**According to the conversation above, who refuses the invitation by making an excuse about a family event?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Andy
- B) Carl
- C) Betty
- D) David

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: B.** Carl, büyükanne ve büyükbabasının ziyarete geleceğini söyleyerek **ailevi bir mazeret** (family event) sunup daveti reddetmiştir. Andy ders projesini mazeret göstermiş, David ise film sevmediğini belirtmiştir.

## 2. Soru Tipi: Unit 2 - Teen Life / Personal Preferences & Survey Analysis

**Soru Kökü:** 100 teenagers answered a survey about their music preferences. Here are the results:
* **Pop Music:** 45%
* **Rock Music:** 30%
* **Classical Music:** 15%
* **Techno Music:** 10%

**Which of the following is CORRECT according to the survey results above?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Classical music is the most popular type of music among teenagers.
- B) Teenagers prefer listening to techno music to rock music.
- C) Nearly half of the teenagers enjoy listening to pop music.
- D) Listening to rock music is less popular than classical music.

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: C.** Pop müzik %45 oranındadır. %45, "yaklaşık olarak yarısı" (nearly half) anlamına geldiği için C seçeneği doğrudur. *(A: Pop en popülerdir; B: Rock, techno'dan daha popülerdir; D: Rock, klasikten daha popülerdir).*

## 3. Soru Tipi: Unit 3 - In The Kitchen / Recipe & Sequence Words

**Soru Kökü:** 
* **First,** peel and slice three apples.  
* **Second,** put the sliced apples into a large bowl and add some sugar and cinnamon.  
* **Next,** mix all the ingredients well with a spoon.  
* **After that,** pour the mixture into a baking tray.  
* **Finally,** bake it in the oven at 180°C for 30 minutes.  

**What should you do IMMEDIATELY BEFORE pouring the mixture into the baking tray?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Peel and slice three apples.
- B) Mix all the ingredients well with a spoon.
- C) Bake it in the oven for 30 minutes.
- D) Add some sugar and cinnamon.

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: B.** Karışımı tepsiye dökmeden **hemen önce** (immediately before) yapılması gereken işlem "Next" adımındaki malzemeleri kaşıkla iyice karıştırmaktır (Mix all the ingredients well).

## 4. Soru Tipi: Unit 4 - On The Phone / Phone Conversation & Leaving a Message

**Soru Kökü:** 
**Secretary:** Good morning, Green Corporation. How can I help you?  
**Mr. Parker:** Hello! Could I speak to Mr. Adams, please?  
**Secretary:** I'm sorry, but he is in a meeting right now. ----?  
**Mr. Parker:** Yes, please. Could you tell him to call me back as soon as possible?  

**Which of the following completes the conversation best?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Would you like to leave a message
- B) Can you hold on a moment, please
- C) Who is calling, please
- D) Why don't you call him later

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: A.** Mr. Parker'ın "Yes, please. Could you tell him to call me..." cevabından anlaşıldığı üzere sekreter **"Would you like to leave a message?"** (Mesaj bırakmak ister misiniz?) sorusunu sormuştur.

## 5. Soru Tipi: Unit 5 - The Internet / Internet Safety Rules

**Soru Kökü:** 
**INTERNET SAFETY RULES**
* Do not share your password with anyone except your parents.  
* Refuse stranger's friend requests on social media.  
* Don't agree to meet up with people you met online.  
* Check if a website is secure before buying something.  

**According to the rules above, who is NOT following the Internet safety rules?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Mark creates strong passwords and keeps them secret.
- B) Sarah ignores friend requests from people she doesn't know.
- C) Tom accepts an invitation to meet a guy he met on a chat room.
- D) Julia asks her parents before downloading a file from the Internet.

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: C.** Güvenlik kurallarından biri "İnternette tanıştığın kişilerle buluşmayı kabul etme" kuralıdır. Tom ise sohbet odasında tanıştığı kişiyle buluşmayı kabul ederek kuralı ihlal etmiştir.

## 6. Soru Tipi: Unit 6 - Adventures / Comparing Extreme Sports (Seçici Soru)

**Soru Kökü:** 

| Feature | Skydiving | Trekking |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Danger** | High | Low |
| **Cost** | Expensive | Cheap |
| **Equipment** | Parachute, helmet | Hiking boots |
| **Popularity** | Very popular | Popular |

**Which of the following is INCORRECT according to the table?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) Skydiving is more dangerous than trekking.
- B) You need fewer equipments for trekking than skydiving.
- C) Trekking is cheaper than skydiving.
- D) Skydiving is less popular than trekking.

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: D.** Tabloda Skydiving "Very popular", Trekking ise "Popular" olarak verilmiştir. Bu durumda Skydiving trekking'den daha az popülerdir (less popular) demek **yanlıştır**.

## 7. Soru Tipi: Unit 7 - Tourism / Visual Brochure Analysis ("NOT answered" Kalıbı)

**Soru Kökü:** 
**GÖBEKLİTEPE: THE ZERO POINT IN HISTORY**
Göbeklitepe is an ancient temple located in Şanlıurfa, Turkey. It is about 12,000 years old, which makes it older than the Pyramids of Egypt. Every year, thousands of tourists visit this fascinating archaeological site to see the giant T-shaped limestone pillars. The best time to visit Göbeklitepe is during spring or autumn when the weather is warm and pleasant.

**Which of the following questions does NOT have an answer in the text?**

**Şıklar:**
- A) How much does it cost to enter Göbeklitepe?
- B) Where is Göbeklitepe located?
- C) How old is the archaeological site?
- D) What is the best season to visit Göbeklitepe?

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: A.** Metinde yer (Şanlıurfa), yaş (12.000 yıllık) ve en iyi ziyaret dönemi (ilkbahar/sonbahar) yanıtlanmıştır. Ancak **Giriş ücreti** (How much does it cost) hakkında bilgi yoktur.

## 9. Soru Tipi: Unit 9 - Science / Scientific Inventions & Biography

**Soru Kökü:** Nikola Tesla was a famous Serbian-American inventor and electrical engineer. He was born in 1856. He made great contributions to the development of alternating current (AC) electricity system. He also worked on wireless communication and X-ray technology. Throughout his life, he registered more than 300 patents for his inventions.

**According to the text, Nikola Tesla ----.**

**Şıklar:**
- A) received only one patent during his career
- B) conducted studies on electricity and wireless communication
- C) was born in the twentieth century
- D) stopped working on science after inventing the X-ray

**Doğru Cevap ve Çözüm:** **Cevap: B.** Metinde Tesla'nın alternatif akım elektriği (electricity) ve kablosuz iletişim (wireless communication) üzerine çalıştığı açıkça ifade edilmiştir.
